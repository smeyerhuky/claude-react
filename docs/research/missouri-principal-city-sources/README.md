Eight official principal-city memberships across seven qualifying CBSAs are
accounted for separately. St. Charles and St. Louis share a CBSA, but each retains
its own publication. Kansas City, Missouri is not substituted for Kansas City,
Kansas or the other Kansas principal cities. Springfield means Missouri; a
same-named out-of-state parking authority is not used.

Five publications were inspected through successful direct HTTP captures; three
through the web renderer after direct HTTP 403. The latter remain explicitly
distinct from successful original-response captures. Full HTML/PDF, app/form
state, error bodies and environment configuration are not published. Response
hashes identify the original attempted captures, not reproducible hashes of the
derived short observations. Capture date is not a field review date.
