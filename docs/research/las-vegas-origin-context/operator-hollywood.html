Hollywood

Boulder Sand & Gravel is an aggregate materials supplier and producer. At the Hollywood pit, we mine and process Type II gravel base. We recycle concrete and asphalt with native material to make a custom blend of different Type II gravel bases. Material accepted for dumping are asphalt, concrete and dirt.
