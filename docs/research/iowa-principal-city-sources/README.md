# Iowa principal-city publication work

Nine exact official principal-city memberships span seven CBSAs: Ames, Cedar
Rapids, Davenport, Des Moines, West Des Moines, Iowa City, Sioux City, Cedar Falls
and Waterloo. Des Moines/West Des Moines and Waterloo/Cedar Falls retain separate
records within their shared metros. A metro title is not a city source key.

Ordinary HTTPS captures on November 8, 2026 UTC inspected seven selected primary
publications. The West Des Moines amphitheater page and Iowa City parking-facility
page returned HTTP 403. Both remain explicit retrieval gaps, with empty quotes
and observations. Search text is not promoted into inspected evidence, and a
blocked request is not a finding that vehicle access is unavailable.

Ames distinguishes employee tags, parking exclusions, resident permits and
reserved spaces. Park Cedar Rapids is an operator source; its listing marks
Five Seasons Ramp closed for ongoing maintenance. General opening/payment
wording does not make that facility open. Davenport distinguishes prepaid
short-term parking from monthly resident, signed and unassigned arrangements.
Des Moines Council Communication 25-012 is dated January 13, 2025 and proposes
code amendments and a future reservation service; this source does not verify
adoption, implementation or current entry conditions. Sioux City's payment
windows and skywalk information do not establish a motor connector. Cedar
Falls' shared business lots and time limits do not establish unrestricted city
road access or gate hours. Waterloo's clerk address and office hours identify
municipal contact context, not a reviewed driveway.

Only short literal quotes, limited observations and external-response
fingerprints are retained. The fingerprints cannot be reconstructed from these
derived records. Raw HTML/PDF, error bodies, application state, private archives
and environment configuration remain outside the repository. Embedded maps and
entrance images were not visually audited. Capture timestamps are not independent
access-review dates. No fixed-scenario permissions are compiled, no anchor is
adopted, no connector is approved, no metro is newly routed and no geographic
coverage or public release is certified. #137's independent reviewer and different
release approver remain required; #138 and #139 remain open.
