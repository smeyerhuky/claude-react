Model assumptions and exact evidence

The adopted instant is fixed at 2026-10-28T18:00:00Z (12:00 MDT). The Thursday case is a regression test. Access is frozen for the whole route; solo ordinary passenger car, no special permits or HOV exemption, tolls allowed. Conditions may change before arrival.

Distances use a sphere of radius 6,371,000 m without elevation. Time uses static posted limits or explicit highway defaults, with no traffic, turn/intersection, acceleration or surface delays. The incoming secondary_link uses the 30 km/h model default; the outgoing way uses raw 30 mph. Speeds are model inputs, not independently verified operating speeds.

Missing incident/exterior roads, other restrictions, zone geography, physical turn/grade/access evidence, reviewed anchors, masks and release assets remain unverified. All public release gates remain closed.
